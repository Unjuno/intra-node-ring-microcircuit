# Evidence report — 2026-08-03

## Experimental question

Does replacing scalar hidden units with five-subnode recurrent ring microcircuits improve predictive performance under matched trainable FP32 parameter budgets?

## Design

- Datasets: Digits, Breast Cancer Wisconsin, Wine, and a controlled synthetic ring-interaction teacher.
- Seeds: 10 paired seeds: `11,22,33,44,55,66,77,88,99,111`.
- Models: MLP, micro-unit with internal edges disabled, and ring micro-unit with three shared recurrent steps.
- Optimizer: AdamW, learning rate `2e-3`, weight decay `1e-4`.
- Selection: validation-loss early stopping, maximum 40 epochs, patience 6.
- Statistics: paired mean difference, Student-t 95% CI, Cohen's paired effect size, and exact sign-flip test over all `2^10` sign assignments.
- Hardware measurement: CPU, batch 256, PyTorch CPU backend. Absolute timing is machine-specific.

## Parameter matching

| Dataset | MLP parameters | Microcircuit parameters | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Digits | 7,402 | 7,402 | 0 |
| Breast cancer | 10,117 | 10,123 | +6 |
| Wine | 10,326 | 10,328 | +2 |
| Synthetic ring | 13,653 | 13,653 | 0 |

The breast-cancer mismatch is six FP32 scalars (approximately 0.059%); the Wine mismatch is two scalars (approximately 0.019%). Results must therefore be described as exact or near-exact matching, not universally exact matching.

A Holm correction over the four `MicroRing − MLP` tests retains the positive breast-cancer result and the two negative results. Digits remains inconclusive.

## Ring-specific causal contrast

The essential contrast is `MicroRing − MicroNoEdges`, because both models have the same internal-state count and trainable parameter count.

| Dataset | Ring-specific difference | 95% CI | Exact p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Breast cancer | −0.09 pp | `[−0.78,+0.60]` | 1.000 |
| Digits | +0.42 pp | `[+0.10,+0.73]` | 0.0293 |
| Synthetic ring | +0.01 pp | `[−0.84,+0.86]` | 1.000 |
| Wine | −0.28 pp | `[−1.41,+0.85]` | 1.000 |

The nominal Digits result does not survive a four-dataset Holm correction. Therefore the completed experiment does not establish a ring-topology advantage.

## Representation and efficiency

- MicroRing had higher normalized effective rank than MLP on Digits and breast cancer, but lower normalized effective rank on Wine and synthetic ring. “Representation expansion” is not a general result.
- CPU latency was approximately 14–18 times the MLP latency in this implementation. Static parameter matching does not imply compute matching.
- The synthetic teacher deliberately used cyclic local interactions, yet MLP still outperformed the microcircuit. Architecture alignment alone is insufficient evidence.

## Change from the earlier five-seed pilot

The earlier pilot suggested a positive Digits effect. The stronger replication changed the parameter budget, generalized the topology implementation, doubled the seed count, and added an architecture-aligned synthetic control. The Digits advantage did not replicate against MLP. This supersedes the earlier positive claim.

## Verdict

- **PASS:** trainability; matched-budget implementation; task-dependent effects; useful falsifiable architecture.
- **FAIL:** general MLP superiority; current compute efficiency; universal representation expansion.
- **UNCERTAIN:** ring-specific benefit; intervention value; anomaly-localization value; topology-specific inductive bias.
